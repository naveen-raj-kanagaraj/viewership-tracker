# Metric definitions

One definition per number, used everywhere on the site (pages, rankings, share cards, exports). Times are IST; a day runs midnight to midnight.

## Live audience

| Metric | Definition | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Watching now | Sum of current viewers of every tracked live stream (each platform's own count) | Twitch worldwide counts every live stream (full count every 5 min); YouTube is a floor (no complete list exists) |
| Peak | Highest "watching now" at one moment, per day, stream, channel or event | |
| Hours watched | Viewers integrated over time live | The standard industry measure of attention |
| Average viewers | Hours watched / hours live | |
| Hours live | Time a channel was seen live | |
| Official vs watch-party share | At a moment, official broadcast viewers / all viewers of the event | Watch parties = permitted co-streams on the organizer's list |

## Channels

| Metric | Definition | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Subscribers / followers | The platform's public count, read daily | YouTube rounds large counts |
| Lifetime views | YouTube's public channel view count | Rejected if below the channel's biggest video |
| Views gained (day, 7 d, 30 d) | Change in lifetime views between daily readings, divided by the days between them | Starts from the day a channel is first followed; YouTube publishes no history |
| Views, last N hours | Change between readings taken during the day | Shown until the first full day is available |
| Engagement | (likes + comments) / views over the posts in the range | |
| Posts per week | Posts in range / weeks in range | Videos, Shorts and live streams; premieres count as videos |
| vs median | A post's views / the median views of the channel's posts in the range | Shows which posts broke out |

## Moments

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| Moment | A jump in viewers of at least 8% and 1,000 people over 1 to 2 minutes, far beyond the stream's normal swings, and/or a chat burst far above its normal rate. News and devotional streams excluded. |
| Hype | Chat z-score + viewer z-score at the moment |
